Curriculum

What the four weeks look like

A steady climb — each week builds directly on the last, ending in a small analytics project of your own.

Week 01

Python foundations

Variables, control flow, functions, and the habits of writing readable code from day one.

Week 02

NumPy & Pandas

Working with arrays and data frames — cleaning, filtering, and reshaping real datasets.

Week 03

Visualization

Turning tables into stories with Matplotlib and Seaborn, and knowing which chart to reach for.

Week 04

Mini analytics project

Applying everything to a dataset end-to-end, with feedback from KDAG mentors along the way.

About the society

Why it's run by KDAG

The Kharagpur Data Analytics Group brings together students across the Institute who are curious about data and machine learning — through workshops, projects, and events built for people encountering the field for the first time. This bootcamp is the same spirit, condensed into four weeks.

  • 01

    Taught by students, for students

    Sessions are run by KDAG members who were in your seat not long ago, so nothing is assumed.

  • 02

    Small, project-driven groups

    Less lecture, more building — each module ends with something you've actually made.

  • 03

    A way into the community

    Bootcamp participants get first access to KDAG's ongoing projects and socials.
